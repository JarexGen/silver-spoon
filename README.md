# Silver Spoon

A VCMI mod for players who want their heroes spoon-fed.

## Submods

- **Level Scaling** - every level, each hero gains: +20% experience gain, +1 Attack/Defense/Spell Power/Knowledge, +1% double-damage chance, ignore 1% of enemy defense, +2% spell damage, +100 gold per day, +10% land movement and +100 sea movement points (all multiplied by hero level). Rough terrain also costs 50 fewer movement points per tile, like Advanced Pathfinding. Heroes whose specialty already scales experience keep only their own bonus.
- **Unlimited Secondary Skills** - removes the 8-skill limit.

## Requirements

VCMI 1.7.0 or newer.
