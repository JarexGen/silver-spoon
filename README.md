# Silver Spoon

A VCMI mod for players who want their heroes spoon-fed.

## Submods

- **Level Scaling** - every level, each hero gains: +20% experience gain, +1 Attack/Defense/Spell Power/Knowledge, +1% double-damage chance, ignore 1% of enemy defense, +2% spell damage, +100 gold per day and +2% land movement (all multiplied by hero level). Heroes whose specialty already scales experience keep only their own bonus.
- **Unlimited Secondary Skills** - removes the 8-skill limit.

## Requirements

VCMI 1.7.0 or newer.
